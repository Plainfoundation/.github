# About us

[Plain](https://plain.com) is the largest block coding community that exists and will be. The Plain Foundation is the largest network of Plain and its users that created Plain in the first place, and ever will be. Millions of people use Plain, so be kind on the platform.

## Come visit the editor!
You can visit the editor at <https://plain.com/editor>. It has a tutorial section that teaches you how to build projects, and a save button in case you lose your data!

## About our community 
This is a space built on kindness, care, and respect.
We listen to one another, support one another, and celebrate each person’s voice.
You belong here — and together, we create a place where everyone feels safe and valued.

We show up for one another with empathy, patience, and understanding. Every voice matters, every story is valued, and every person deserves to feel safe and respected. We believe in listening before speaking, helping before judging, and uplifting before criticizing. When someone succeeds, we celebrate. When someone struggles, we support. When someone speaks, we honor their perspective.This is a place where compassion guides our actions, respect shapes our conversations, and care connects us as a community.
